# Privacy policy

Effective 12 October 2026. This policy covers Sprint Pack, an app for Jira Cloud sold on the Atlassian Marketplace by Weave Commons ("we").

## Where your data lives

Sprint Pack runs on Atlassian's Forge platform and has no server of its own. Everything it reads and stores stays in Atlassian's cloud, with your Jira site. We don't receive a copy of it.

## What the app stores

In Atlassian's storage for your site:

- **Sprint history:** for each sprint, its dates and state, and which issues joined, left, were completed or carried over, with their estimates and statuses. Issues are stored by ID and key; their summaries and descriptions are not stored.
- **Who made a change:** the Atlassian account ID of the person who moved an issue in or out of a sprint, published or unpublished BI data, changed BI labels, or dismissed an inferred reopen.
- **Display names:** the names of the people above, cached so the admin page can show them, and read again from Jira after a day.
- **Access caches:** which boards a viewer can see, keyed by account ID, kept for up to an hour so pages load faster.
- **Settings and status:** the app's settings, its point budget and the state of its background work.

In Jira itself: the app's read-only fields on issues, its stored JQL search results, and, if an admin publishes BI data, sprint totals saved as board properties.

The app doesn't collect email addresses, passwords or API tokens.

## Logs

Atlassian's platform keeps the app's logs. They hold issue, sprint and board IDs and error messages, not account IDs. We read them only to support and fix the app.

## What leaves Atlassian

Nothing, by the app. Files you export are built in your browser and saved by you. BI data you pull into another tool with your own credentials is your export.

## What we receive

- From the Atlassian Marketplace: the licence and contact details Atlassian shares with app vendors, such as your site and your billing and technical contacts. We use them for licensing, billing questions and support.
- From you: whatever you send us when you ask for support.

We don't sell personal data and don't use it for advertising.

## How long data is kept

While the app is installed. When it's uninstalled, the app deletes the BI data it published, and Atlassian keeps the app's storage for 28 days before deleting it. Support emails are kept as long as needed to answer them, and at most 2 years.

## Your rights

For data in your Jira site, contact your site admin, who can uninstall the app. For anything we hold, email support@sprintpack.weavecommons.com.
