# Support

Email support@sprintpack.weavecommons.com. We reply within 2 business days (Monday to Friday, US Pacific time), and sooner for anything that stops the app working.

It helps to include:

- your Jira site address;
- the board and sprint, and what you expected to see;
- a screenshot of the admin page's "Needs attention" section, if it lists anything.

Many answers are already in the [help pages](index.md). For security issues, see [Security](security.md).
